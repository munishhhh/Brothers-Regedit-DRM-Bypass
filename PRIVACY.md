# Privacy Policy for Brothers Regedit DRM Bypass

**Last Updated:** February 9, 2026

## Data Collection

**Brothers Regedit DRM Bypass does NOT collect, store, or transmit any user data.**

## What the Extension Does

This extension:
- Applies CSS compositing styling to video elements on supported streaming sites
- Stores your enable/disable preference locally in your browser's localStorage
- Does not access, collect, or transmit any personal information
- Does not track your browsing history
- Does not access your streaming account information
- Does not communicate with any external servers

## Permissions Explained

### scripting
Used to inject CSS styling into supported streaming pages to fix the black screen issue during screen sharing.

### activeTab
Allows the extension to interact with the active streaming tab when you click the extension icon.

### host_permissions
Required to access supported streaming pages and apply the CSS fix.
## Local Storage

The extension uses browser localStorage to remember your on/off preference. This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not accessible to any external parties
- Can be cleared by disabling or removing the extension

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.
